# Hearth and Rye ordering page

Hearth and Rye on Baker Street has a new ordering page that replaces the paper pad by the register, and Mina Cole is the baker who runs the weekday bake. Customers can order the country loaf for $6, or they can order the sesame bun for $3, or they can order the morning rye for $5 when they want bread from that page.

The sesame bun is topped with seeds, and the page shows that next to the bun so people can read it before they order. Pickup runs from 8 AM to 11 AM on Tuesday through Friday, while orders close at 6 PM the day before the pickup the customer asked for.

No account is required for a person who only wants one loaf, and the page shows a name field plus a pickup day field plus a note field on the same screen. Mina Cole bakes 40 loaves each weekday, and the page is where those loaves are offered to the people who ordered ahead.

Mina Cole reported that 80% of regulars want a page instead of the pad they use before work, and the ordering page is the one she put up for that weekday crowd.
